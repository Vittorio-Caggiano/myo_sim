myo_sim.build.references.MOBL_ARM holds fibre length, tendon slack and
pennation transcribed from the MoBL-ARMS upper-limb model (Holzbaur et al.
2005 Ann Biomed Eng 33:829-840, doi:10.1007/s10439-005-3320-7; Saul et al.
2015 Comput Methods Biomech Biomed Engin 18:1445-1458,
doi:10.1080/10255842.2014.916698).

These are derived parameter values, not a copy of MOBL_ARMS_41.osim. The
.osim is not redistributed. The SimTK MoBL-ARMS release is BSD-3 with a
non-commercial restriction; a BSD-3 copy of the OpenSim model is also at
https://github.com/CEINMS-RT/UpperLimbModel.
