myo_sim.build.references.RAJAGOPAL2016_LEG holds fibre length, tendon slack
and pennation transcribed from Rajagopal et al. 2016 (IEEE Trans Biomed Eng
63:2068-2079, doi:10.1109/TBME.2016.2586891), Table II / the Rajagopal2016
OpenSim model on SimTK (https://simtk.org/projects/full_body).

These are derived parameter values, not a copy of Rajagopal2016.osim. The
.osim is not redistributed. Use of the published table values follows the
paper's citation; the SimTK model license is Apache-2.0.
